## Contact us

Email us at **[{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject={{ page.app | append: " support" | uri_escape }})**. We usually reply within 2 business days.

To help us solve your problem faster, please include:

- What happened, and what you expected to happen
- Your device model and iOS version
- The app version (shown in **[Settings → About]** in the app)
- Screenshots, if they help

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app, go to **[Settings → Restore Purchases]**, and sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy them.

### How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and choose {{ page.app }}.

### How do I delete my account and data?

Go to **[Settings → Delete Account]** in the app, or email us and we’ll do it for you. See our [privacy policy](../privacy/) for details.
