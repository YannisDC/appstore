{{ page.app }} is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how {{ site.company }} (“we”, “us”) handles information in the app. In short: everything you do in {{ page.app }} stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers or to third parties. {{ page.app }} has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection of its own.

## Information we do not collect

{{ page.app }} does not collect, store, or transmit the following to our servers or third parties:

- Personal information (name, address, email, phone number, etc.)
- Health information about you, your partner or your baby
- Your notes or the cards you play
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Any data about how you use the app

## Data stored on your device

To remember where you are in the deck, the app stores the following in its settings on your device:

- **Your progress:** which cards are in play or done, the steps you’ve ticked, and when you started or finished a card
- **Your notes:** anything you write in a card’s notes
- **Recently opened cards** and the last card you drew
- **Your local helpline:** the helpline name and number and the emergency number used by *When to Reach Out*. The app suggests a well-known service for your region; you can change it in Settings.
- **Whether you’ve seen the welcome screens**

This data never leaves your device through the app. It is used only to show your progress, notes and helpline in the app.

### Device backups

The app’s data may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), according to your device settings. These backups are handled by Apple or by you and are subject to Apple’s privacy policies. We do not have access to them.

## Permissions

{{ page.app }} does not ask for any permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, health data or notifications.

## Calling and sharing

- **Calling:** when you tap a call button in *When to Reach Out*, iOS asks you to confirm and then places the call with the Phone app. The app does not record or log calls.
- **Sharing:** when you share a card or a message with your partner, it is sent directly from your device to the destination you choose (e.g. Messages, Mail, AirDrop) using iOS sharing. We do not facilitate or have access to these transfers.

## Third-party services

The app uses only Apple frameworks that run on your device, and the App Store for distribution, both subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children

{{ page.app }} is written for adults, mainly expectant and new fathers. It is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

## Health information

{{ page.app }} offers practical suggestions for new parents. It is not a medical device and does not replace your midwife, doctor or health visitor. Nothing you enter in the app is used to give medical advice.

## Security

Since all your data is stored on your device, its security is mainly provided by iOS and your device settings. We recommend using a passcode or Face ID and keeping iOS up to date, especially if your notes are personal.

## Your rights

You have full control over your data:

- **Delete progress and notes:** in the app, open Settings (the gear on the Start or Reach Out tab) and tap **Reset all progress**
- **Change your helpline:** edit it in Settings at any time
- **Uninstall:** uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device (device backups remain)

Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you may have further rights over your personal data. Since we hold none, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete, but you’re welcome to contact us with any question.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the app or its App Store listing.
