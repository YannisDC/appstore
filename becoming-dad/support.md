Need help with {{ page.app }}? We’re happy to help.

**If you or your partner are struggling right now,** open the **Reach Out** tab in the app, call your doctor, or call your local emergency number. Don’t wait for an email reply.

## Contact us

Email us at **[{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject={{ page.app | append: " support" | uri_escape }})**. We usually reply within 2 business days.

To help us solve your problem faster, please include:

- What happened, and what you expected to happen
- Your device model and iOS version
- Screenshots, if they help

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I start?

On the **Start** tab, pick the line that sounds like you this week, such as “The baby’s on the way” or “Nobody’s sleeping”. It takes you to the cards for that stage. Not sure? Tap **Draw a card** and let the deck pick one.

### How do I see the steps on a card?

Tap the card to turn it over, or tap the turn-over button under it. On iPad, the front and back are shown side by side. Swipe left or right to move to the other cards of the same colour.

### How do I track a card?

Tap **Play this week** to put a card in play, then tap each step on the back to tick it off. Tap **Mark as done** when you’ve played it. Everything in play or done is listed on the **My Moves** tab.

### What are recipes?

Recipes put five cards in a sensible order for a moment in your journey, such as *Before the Birth* or *The First Week*. Open the **Recipes** tab, tap **Start this recipe**, and spend a few days on each card before moving to the next.

### Can I write my own notes?

Yes. On any card, tap the notes button next to the main button. Notes are saved on your device and listed on the **My Moves** tab.

### How do I set my local helpline?

Open Settings (the gear on the **Start** or **Reach Out** tab) and enter your helpline’s name and number and your emergency number. The app suggests a well-known service for your region, so please check it’s right for where you live.

### How do I start again?

Open Settings and tap **Reset all progress**. This clears cards in play, done cards, ticked steps and notes. To see the welcome screens again, tap **Show the welcome again**.

### Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Your progress and notes stay on your device, and {{ page.app }} has no network connection of its own. See our [privacy policy](../privacy/) for details.

### Can {{ page.app }} replace my midwife or doctor?

No. The cards support you. They don’t replace your midwife, doctor or health visitor. If something worries you about your partner’s or your baby’s health, contact them directly.
